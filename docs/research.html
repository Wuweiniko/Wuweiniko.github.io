Research

Biomarkers of multiple sclerosis progression.

Primary research interest

My primary scientific interest is identifying and evaluating biological markers of progression in multiple sclerosis. I am interested in how these markers can help characterize the processes underlying disability accumulation and improve the assessment of disease progression.

Research experience

Cognition and clinical assessment

At the Saint Petersburg Centre of Multiple Sclerosis and AID, I contributed to the Russian validation of the Brief International Cognitive Assessment for Multiple Sclerosis (BICAMS), with responsibilities spanning study design, data collection, and analysis. I also led work on the Russian validation of an electronic Expanded Disability Status Scale for a national digital multiple sclerosis platform.

Comorbidity and disability

I studied comorbidity in people with multiple sclerosis using validated self-report questionnaires. This work included research and presentations on cardiovascular comorbidity and its relationship to disability.

Treatment outcomes and clinical trials

My research experience includes real-world evaluation of natalizumab and participation as a co-investigator in phase III clinical trials. I contributed to research on the efficacy and safety of siponimod in the Russian population with secondary progressive multiple sclerosis.
